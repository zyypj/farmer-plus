# Avocado tree

**English** | [Português](avocado.pt-BR.md)

A full-size fruit tree with a complete, vanilla-compatible wood set. Wild avocado trees grow in jungles; their leaves flower, set fruit and ripen again and again.

## The tree

Modelled on real avocado trees (*Persea americana*): 9–20 m tall, a short trunk that splits low into 3–5 main scaffold limbs, and a **rounded, very dense crown** often as wide as it is tall, with foliage reaching low ([UF/IFAS](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/ST435), [Missouri Botanical Garden](https://www.missouribotanicalgarden.org/PlantFinder/PlantFinderDetails.aspx?taxonid=281661), [World Agroforestry](https://apps.worldagroforestry.org/treedb/AFTPDFS/Persea_americana.PDF), [California Avocado Growers](https://www.californiaavocadogrowers.com/sites/default/files/2023-11/Architecture_of_Avo_Trees.pdf)).

| Tree | How you get it | Height | Crown | Trunk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orchard | One sapling | 10–13 blocks | ≈11–13 wide | 10 px trunk, 2–3 blocks clear |
| Wild | **2×2 saplings** (like giant jungle trees) and jungles | 16–20 blocks | ≈15–17 wide | 1 log block wide, 3–5 blocks clear |

![Orchard avocado tree](images/avocado-orchard-tree.png) ![Wild avocado tree](images/avocado-wild-tree.png) ![Orchard tree without leaves](images/avocado-tree-wood.png)

*Renders of trees generated by the game and read back from the saved world (`python art/preview_world_trees.py`).*

- **Thin wood:** a real avocado trunk is only 30–60 cm across and its limbs are thinner still, so the tree is not built from full logs. `avocado_trunk` (10 px) forms the trunk and the central leader, `avocado_branch` (6 px) forms every limb, and both connect to each other, to logs and to the ground like chorus plants or fences (`core/wood/BranchBlock`). Only the old wild giant keeps a full `avocado_log` at its base.
- **Shape:** generated by `farmerplus:broadleaf_tree` (`core/worldgen/BroadleafTreeFeature`): a central leader plus 3–5 limbs at random angles, a side branch and a low "skirt" branch on every limb, and a leaf cloud (flattened ellipsoid with a ragged edge) around every branch tip. Every leaf is within 6 blocks of wood, so nothing decays.
- **Tuning:** `data/farmerplus/worldgen/feature/avocado_tree.json` and `wild_avocado_tree.json` expose the `trunk`, `leader` and `branch` blocks plus `height`, `trunk_height`, `limbs` and `crown_radius`; any future broadleaf tree (mango, orange…) reuses the same feature with its own JSON.
- **Sapling:** grows at half the vanilla speed (≈1 in 14 random ticks per stage, light 9+) and needs room for the whole crown.
- **Wild trees:** sparse jungle, jungle and bamboo jungle (`farmerplus:has_avocado_trees`), about one tree every six chunks.

## Fruiting

Only **natural** leaves (placed by the tree, not by players) bear fruit, and only those with **air below** and on a sparse pattern, so a mature tree carries a realistic handful of avocados (about 5% of its leaves).

The fruit is its own block, **`avocado_fruit`**, hanging in the air block under the leaf (like cocoa on jungle logs). It has a real hitbox that you can aim at, it never falls by itself, and it breaks if the leaf above it is removed.

| Stage | `stage` | Looks like | Next stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | Plain leaves | A leaf grows flowers below it (≈1 in 24 random ticks) |
| Flowering | 0 | Small yellow-green flowers | Green fruit (≈1 in 8) |
| Unripe | 1 | Small green avocado | Ripe (≈1 in 10) |
| Ripe | 2 | Large dark avocado | Stays until picked |

With the default `random_tick_speed`, the first ripe avocado shows up about 7–11 minutes after a tree is grown; a single spot takes about 48 minutes from leaf to ripe fruit.

- **Pick:** right-click a ripe avocado to get it; the spot is free again and the leaf flowers later, so a tree fruits many times. Bone meal advances one stage.
- **Breaking** a ripe avocado also drops it; flowers and green fruit drop nothing. Leaves drop saplings (5%) and **sticks** like oak leaves; shears or Silk Touch give the leaves block.
- Avocado: 4 hunger, 0.6 saturation; villagers eat it; compostable.

## Wood set

`avocado_log`, `avocado_wood`, `stripped_avocado_log`, `stripped_avocado_wood`, `avocado_planks`, `avocado_stairs`, `avocado_slab`, `avocado_fence`, `avocado_fence_gate`, `avocado_door`, `avocado_trapdoor`, `avocado_pressure_plate`, `avocado_button`, `avocado_sign` (+ `avocado_wall_sign`), `avocado_hanging_sign` (+ `avocado_wall_hanging_sign`), `avocado_shelf`, `avocado_boat`, `avocado_chest_boat`, `avocado_leaves`, `avocado_sapling`.

The tree itself is made of two connecting blocks that are not part of the vanilla set: **`avocado_trunk`** drops one `avocado_log` and **`avocado_branch`** drops 1–2 sticks (silk touch keeps either block). Both are axe-mineable, burn like logs, keep nearby leaves alive (`minecraft:prevents_nearby_leaf_decay`) and can be placed by hand to build your own trees.

The set mirrors oak everywhere it matters:

- **Tags:** every vanilla tag that lists an oak block lists the avocado equivalent (`minecraft:logs_that_burn`, `minecraft:planks`, `minecraft:wooden_*`, `minecraft:fence_gates`, `minecraft:standing_signs`, `minecraft:wall_signs`, `minecraft:ceiling_hanging_signs`, `minecraft:wall_hanging_signs`, `minecraft:wooden_shelves`, `minecraft:boats`, `minecraft:chest_boats`, `minecraft:leaves`, `minecraft:saplings`, entity type `minecraft:boat`, …). Planks therefore work in **every** wooden recipe — crafting tables, sticks, chests, tools, barrels — and logs work in campfires, charcoal and so on.
- **Recipes and loot:** same as oak (planks from `#farmerplus:avocado_logs`, wood, stairs, slabs, fences, gates, doors, trapdoors, plates, buttons, signs, hanging signs, shelves, boats and chest boats).
- **Stripping:** right-click a log or wood with any axe (`#minecraft:axes`). The log handles it itself, so no shared vanilla file is overridden.
- **Fuel:** same burn times as oak through the `minecraft:cooking_fuel` item component.
- **Fire:** same flammability as oak (shelves burn like vanilla shelves). Fabric and Forge use the vanilla fire table (`FireBlockAccessor`), NeoForge uses its data map (`data/neoforge/data_maps/block/flammable.json`).

### Signs, shelf and boats

- **Wood type:** every set has its own `WoodType` and `BlockSetType`, named `farmerplus/<wood>` (sounds are the oak defaults). The name only picks the sign editing screens: `minecraft:textures/gui/signs/farmerplus/<wood>.png` and `minecraft:textures/gui/hanging_signs/farmerplus/<wood>.png`.
- **Signs, hanging signs and shelf** use the vanilla block entity types `minecraft:sign`, `minecraft:hanging_sign` and `minecraft:shelf`. In 26.x their bodies are ordinary block models and the vanilla renderers only draw text and shelf items, so no renderer is registered. The blocks are added to the vanilla types' valid blocks through `BlockEntityBlocks`: Fabric calls `addValidBlock`, NeoForge uses `BlockEntityTypeAddBlocksEvent`, Forge (no API) uses `BlockEntityTypeAccessor` during common setup.
- **Boats:** `farmerplus:<wood>_boat` and `farmerplus:<wood>_chest_boat` are vanilla `Boat`/`ChestBoat` entities with their own entity types. `client/BoatRendering` registers their model layers (`farmerplus:boat/<wood>`, `farmerplus:chest_boat/<wood>`) and `BoatRenderer`s, called from the Fabric client entrypoint, the NeoForge client `@Mod` and the Forge `EntityRenderersEvent`. Textures: `farmerplus:textures/entity/boat/<wood>.png` and `entity/chest_boat/<wood>.png`. Dispensers place them like vanilla boats.

The chest on a chest boat is always the **vanilla chest**: `client/ChestBoatRenderer` draws the hull with the avocado texture and the chest pieces with the vanilla oak chest boat texture, so no Mojang pixels are copied.

## Reusing the wood set

`WoodSets.register(name, woodColor, barkColor)` registers the 18 wood blocks, 18 items and 2 boat entity types, and adds the set to `WoodSets.all()` (boat renderers and dispenser behaviours follow automatically). `python tools/wood_set.py <name>` generates their blockstates, models, item definitions, loot tables, recipes, recipe unlocks and tag entries (block, item and entity type tags) from the vanilla oak templates. Leaves, sapling, tree feature, textures and lang entries are per tree.
