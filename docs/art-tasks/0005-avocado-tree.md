## Reference: the real avocado tree

- **Bark:** grey-brown, finely fissured into small irregular plates, a little rough; never as dark as dark oak.
- **Wood:** pale, creamy yellow to light pinkish-tan, fine and even grain with few knots.
- **Leaves:** elliptical, leathery, glossy dark green on top, paler underneath, 10–25 cm long, crowded at branch tips; the canopy is dense and dark.
- **Flowers:** tiny, pale yellow-green, in loose clusters at branch tips.
- **Fruit:** pear-shaped, hanging singly or in pairs on a thick stalk. Unripe: bright, smooth green. Ripe (Hass style): dark green to purplish-black, pebbly skin.

## Trunk and branch models (Blockbench, optional upgrade)

The tree is built from two connecting blocks, generated by `python art/avocado_branches.py` as plain boxes that reuse `avocado_log.png` (bark) and `avocado_log_top.png` (cut ends):

| Block | Width | Models (`models/block/`) |
|---|---|---|
| `avocado_trunk` | 10 px | `avocado_trunk_core.json`, `avocado_trunk_arm.json`, `avocado_trunk_inventory.json` |
| `avocado_branch` | 6 px | `avocado_branch_core.json`, `avocado_branch_arm.json`, `avocado_branch_inventory.json` |

The blockstate is multipart: `*_core` is always drawn and `*_arm` (pointing north, from z = 0 to the core) is rotated towards every connected side. You may add bark detail (a knot, a slight flare where an arm meets the core) in Blockbench, but keep each arm inside its box, keep the core centred and keep the file names; if you rerun the script it overwrites them, so stop using it once the models are hand-made.

## Fruit models (Blockbench, optional upgrade)

`models/block/avocado_fruit_flowering.json`, `avocado_fruit_unripe.json` and `avocado_fruit_ripe.json` (sources `art/blockbench/avocado_fruit_*.bbmodel`) belong to the **`avocado_fruit` block**, which sits in the air block under a leaf (`stage` 0–2). They hang from the top face: keep the stalk touching y = 16, everything between y = 8 and y = 16 and inside x/z 0–16, ≤ 8 elements per model. These used to be leaf overlays named `avocado_leaves_*` drawn between y = −8 and 0; the models were moved up by 16 px, so `art/avocado_pixels.py`, `art/export_avocado.js` and `art/preview_avocado.js` must write the new names and coordinates (`python art/validate_avocado_art.py` already checks them).
